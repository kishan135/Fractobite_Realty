Property Insights

Practical guides to help you evaluate property, prepare for a site visit, and compare ownership costs.

Buying

A checklist for your first site visit

Read guide

Bring the floor plan and the list of advertised amenities. Check daylight, ventilation, water supply, parking access, and noise at the property. Confirm which parts of the development are ready to use.

Land

Questions to ask before shortlisting a plot

Read guide

Ask for current title records, approved land use, survey boundaries, legal access, and availability of utilities. Compare the documents with the physical site and seek independent legal review.

Investing

How to compare commercial spaces

Read guide

Compare usable floor area, fit-out costs, access, permitted use, maintenance, and lease conditions. Calculate the costs of owning the space during a vacancy.

Buying

Budget beyond the quoted property price

Read guide

Set aside an estimate for registration, taxes, maintenance deposits, legal checks, and finishing costs. Ask for an itemized cost sheet and verify which costs apply to your purchase.
